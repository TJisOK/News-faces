Face mosaic

Your face, rebuilt live from the news photos above. Each cell shows the photo whose colour is closest to that part of your face. The photos are never altered: the colour sliders shift the target, so different photos get chosen.

Recording captures the live mosaic at up to 1920px and exports an MP4 (H.264) straight from the browser.

Every head is rendered at the same size. Drag a head to move it on its own; drag empty space to pan; pinch or ctrl+scroll zooms all heads together. “Equal size & arrange” snaps them back into a grid. Only photos passing the Filters and Sources above are used. Click a cell to open its photo.

Sources

Through public proxies each section yields about 10 photos per pass, fetched one every few seconds; auto refresh keeps topping the cache up. With python3 serve.py at http://localhost:8765 feeds load in parallel with their full item lists.
